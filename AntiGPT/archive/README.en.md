# Historical Governance

[English](README.en.md) · [简体中文](README.md)

This directory preserves governance documents that preceded AntiGPT.

```text
JDD → TED → COST → AntiGPT
```

- **[JDD](JDD.md)** focused on justified machinery and resistance to speculative engineering.
- **TED** reframed engineering burden through total Token cost: [English](TED.md) · [中文](TED.zh-CN.md).
- **COST** generalized the model to expected total cost across the software trajectory: [English](COST.en.md) · [中文](COST.zh-CN.md).
- **AntiGPT** moved the governing model to Agent behavior itself: judgment, objective preservation, ownership-level correction, bounded investigation, bounded verification, and resistance to defensive cognition.

These files are historical sources. They do not define current repository or Agent policy. Current governance is [`CONSTITUTION.md`](../CONSTITUTION.md) plus the AntiGPT role Skills.

The archived documents are intentionally preserved close to their original form. Historical wording may conflict with current AntiGPT doctrine.
